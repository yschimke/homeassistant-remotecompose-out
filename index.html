HaCard/Tile

HA's tile card: a toggleable light, on. Bound to `light.kitchen`, so its state and accent follow the entity live.
×

HaCard/EntityRow

One row of HA's entities card, or a whole entity card: icon, name and state.
×

HaCard/Metric

A reusable sensor reading with a live value, a separate unit and a custom text color.
×

HaCard/StatusChip

A small colored status indicator whose text and active state can follow an entity.
×

HaCard/Entities

HA's entities card in its simple form: a title over up to three rows.
×

HaCard/Gauge

HA's gauge card: a reading on a half-circle dial between its min and max.
×

HaCard/Thermostat

HA's thermostat card: the room temperature on a 270° dial, the set point marked on it and the HVAC mode above.
×

HaCard/Glance

HA's glance card: a row of icon cells, each an entity's name over its state.
×

HaCard/Heading

HA's heading card: the title a dashboard section opens with.
×

HaCard/Button

HA's button card: a large icon over the entity's name. Toggleable, its accent follows the entity and a tap toggles it.
×

HaCard/Toggle

The toggle switch an entities row ends in, tweening between its two accents.
×

HaCard/Icon

An entity icon by key — light, switch, climate, sensor, fan, lock, … — tinted.
×

HaCard/Surface

The surface a dashboard card sits on — the theme's card background, divider and 12dp inset — here around a heading and a tile. The Dashboard card root exports as this.
×

HaCard/DashboardSection

A dashboard section: a heading over cards, each on its own surface, as HA's sections view stacks them. The Dashboard section root exports as this.
×

Remote/Card

Remote Material 3's RemoteCard, as remote-m3 offers it.
×

HaCard/Template

A card design template: the entity names and card title inside it bind to the values the app pushes (`light.s0.name`, `card.title`), so a design made from it can be published as a card design.
×

HaCard/UberTheme

An uber theme around a card: here the LCD look — segment-drawn thermometer and battery in one ink, monospace text and a square bezel — over the same components as everywhere else.
×

HaCard/UberThemeConsole

The `console` uber theme: a skeuomorphic studio desk. The thermostat is a knurled knob in a ring of lamps, the gauge a backlit VU meter, the thermometer a glass liquid column, all on a lit metal panel.
×

HaCard/ConsoleControls

Console instruments: smoked-glass LED clock, recessed transport keys with separate pilot lamps, a mechanical rocker and an enamel-backed liquid thermometer.
×

HaCard/ConsoleServiceLabels

White service tape labels Console's missing, unsupported and unavailable content, while preserving the original message and live recovery behavior.
×

HaCard/UberThemeIkea

IKEA Showroom: timber-edged frames, rechargeable-cell meters and moulded smart-control paddles. Original product-inspired drawings; portable sans typography.
×

Remote/Button

Remote Material 3's RemoteButton, as remote-m3 offers it: here, running a scene.
×

Remote/SwitchButton

Remote Material 3's RemoteSwitchButton: a labelled switch, checked.
×

Remote/Text

Remote Material 3's RemoteText, set by size: the text every template binds.
×

HaCard/Thermometer

A temperature as a thermometer, standard presentation: the glyph fills along HA's comfort ramp and follows `<entityId>.numeric_state` live.
×

HaCard/Thermometer-Adaptive-Tall

The adaptive thermometer in a tall cell: expanded, the tube grows with the cell and the range appears under a promoted reading.
×

HaCard/Thermometer-Adaptive-Short

The same adaptive thermometer in a Wear Small–shaped cell: compact, the glyph beside the reading and name.
×

HaCard/Thermometer-Chip

The chip rung, for a 1×1 launcher cell: the thermometer over its reading, no name.
×

HaCard/Battery

A battery level as a battery; below 30 % it turns amber, below 15 % red.
×

HaCard/Measurement

A reading drawn by its kind — humidity, on an arc — at the compact presentation.

HaCard/ArcMeter
